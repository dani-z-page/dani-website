Using the logo

  • Pick by background, not by name. The dark logo (dark ink) goes on light/white surfaces — invoices, email, most documents. The light logo (white) goes on dark surfaces — your website, dark slides.
  • Solid vs transparent. The plain PNG comes on its matching background (ink for light, cream for dark). The transparent PNG drops that background out — use it when placing the logo over a color, a photo, or a letterhead.
  • Clear space. Keep empty space around the logo at least as tall as the teal dot on all sides. Don't crowd it.
  • Minimum size. Full logo: ~140px wide. Mark alone: ~24px. Files are ~3000px wide, so they scale down cleanly for print too.
  • Don't recolor it outside the palette, stretch or squash it, rotate it, add effects, or place the white version on a light background (or the dark version on a dark one).
  • Colors: accent teal #2FD4CB on dark / #12867F on light · ink #EEF1F0 (light) / #1A1810 (dark). Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque (800 for ZIMMERMAN, 400 tracked for ADVISORY).

Need it in another format (SVG vector, an ICO favicon, a specific pixel size, or a stacked layout)? Just ask.
