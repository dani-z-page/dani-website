The method · From diagnosis to durable ownership

From urgent problems to an operating model the team can own

The work moves through four phases, with diagnosis continuing as execution begins. I start by stabilizing what cannot wait and understanding how customers, revenue, and work move through the company. Then we build and test the operating model the business needs now, shifting ownership to the permanent team throughout.

01 · DIAGNOSE + STABILIZE

Map customers, revenue, renewals, active implementations, support demand, and customer economics. Trace the end-to-end journey and current ownership while bringing urgent risks and executive escalations under control.

Shared diagnosis and immediate control

02 · DESIGN + BUILD

Define decision rights across the customer-facing teams. Build the segmentation and coverage model, support tiers, implementation standards, escalation paths, and success measures the company needs at its current scale.

A practical model built for the business as it operates today

03 · PILOT + OPERATIONALIZE

Test the model with live customers, renewals, and implementations. Establish the routines for managing customer risk, expansion, product feedback, and resource decisions. Develop or hire the leaders the model requires.

Working practices, evidence, and clear accountability

04 · EMBED + TRANSFER

Scale the practices that prove effective. Coach leaders as they assume decision ownership, then embed the roadmap, metrics, and operating cadence in the permanent organization.

A team equipped to operate independently
